Sanskrit C, as Hindawi writes it, raised to chandah through the sutram layers, lowered back to compilable Sanskrit C, and built and run by Hindawi.

A word for every symbol, katapayadi numerals, samasa, sandhi and chhandas; every Sanskrit C and C++ sample of Hindawi 2026.10 goes through all 26 paths and back, and builds the same. The layers are sutram v11, written by Meta AI under the direction of Abhishek Choudhary, corrected and completed.
